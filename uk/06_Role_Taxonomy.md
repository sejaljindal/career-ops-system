## Part 5 — UK visa coding, one thing to verify

Under Skilled Worker rules your role must sit at **RQF Level 6+** and meet the going rate. The relevant occupation family is **management consultants and business analysts**, where going rates run roughly **£42,900–£51,000** depending on the exact code.

**This matters more than it sounds.** The same job — "Business Analyst" — can be coded into an eligible occupation or an ineligible one depending on how the employer describes the duties. Mis-coding is a common refusal reason.

You already have the gov.uk going-rates page bookmarked in your resources tab group. Open it, find the exact code for the roles you are targeting, and note the going rate. Then when a recruiter asks what you are looking for salary-wise, you can answer with the number that makes sponsorship legally possible rather than a number that quietly kills it.

**Practical consequence:** "PMO Analyst" and "Project Controls Analyst" are the titles in Tier 2 most likely to be coded below RQF 6. "Management Consultant", "Business Analyst" and "Transformation Consultant" are the safest. Weight your applications accordingly — not because they fit you better, but because they are more likely to be legally fillable.
