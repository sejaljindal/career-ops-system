I earlier told you the relevant occupation family was **SOC 2423/2424** at roughly £42,900–£51,000. That was wrong on the code. From the official table:

That is your primary code, and it is worth knowing precisely, because it changes your new entrant floor.

# PART 2 — The thing that decides everything

Look at these two codes side by side:

| | **2431** Management consultants and business analysts | **3543** Project support officers |
|---|---|---|
| Going rate | £50,200 | £33,900 |
| Skill level | RQF 6 — **eligible** | Below RQF 6 — **not eligible for you** |
| Titles it covers | Business analysts and consultants, management advisers, risk analysts | Project support officers |
| Your new entrant floor | £35,140 | *n/a — cannot be sponsored* |

**This is the entire ballgame, and it has nothing to do with industry.**

Since 22 July 2025 new Skilled Worker applications must be at RQF Level 6 or above — which in practice means codes in the **1xxx and 2xxx** ranges. The 3xxx codes still appear in the table because their "lower rate" column applies to people who already held a Certificate of Sponsorship before April 2024. **That is not you.**

So these are closed to you as a new applicant, regardless of what they pay:

• **3543 Project support officers** — £33,900
• **3544 Data analysts** — £34,900
• **3549 Business associate professionals** (includes *Business systems analysts*) — £33,400
• **3557 Events managers and organisers** — £33,400
• **4112 Local government administrative occupations** — £33,400

**Read that list again.** "PMO Analyst", "Project Support Officer", "Data Analyst", "Events Manager" — several of the titles you have been searching hardest sit in codes you cannot be sponsored under at all. Not underpaid. **Ineligible.**

Meanwhile the same person doing recognisably similar work, described as *analysis and advisory* rather than *support and coordination*, codes to 2431 and is fully eligible.

**What to do with this:** your applications and CV need to pull toward 2431-type duties — analysis, advisory, recommendations, decision support — and away from 3543-type duties — support, coordination, administration, organising. Your actual EMPLOYER_ONE work qualifies: you identified a £100K commercial risk, produced executive decision-support, analysed supplier delivery. That is 2431 language. "Team Organiser" is 3543 language.

You do not need to lie. You need to stop under-describing yourself.

# PART 4 — Alternative visa routes

You asked me to look beyond Skilled Worker. Here is all of it, honestly assessed.

## India Young Professionals Scheme — the big one

**This removes the sponsorship problem entirely.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Indian citizens aged **18–30** |
| Requires | Bachelor's degree or above · ~£2,530 savings · no dependent children |
| Gives you | **2 years in the UK, any job, no sponsor, no salary threshold** |
| Places | 3,000 per year, allocated by **random ballot** |
| Cost to enter ballot | Free. You pay the visa fee only if selected |

**You qualify on every criterion.** You are 25, an Indian citizen, and hold both a BBA and a YOUR_MSc.

**The bad news:** the 2026 ballots have both closed. February 2026 held most of the places; the second and final 2026 ballot ran **21–23 July 2026** — three weeks before today. You missed it by less than a month.

**The good news:** the next ballot should open around **February 2027**, and the age limit is 30, so you have roughly five more years of eligibility. This is entirely unaffected by your 26th birthday.

**Do this now:** set a calendar reminder for **1 February 2027** and check the gov.uk page weekly from mid-January. Ballots open and close within a 48-hour window and are not widely announced. Missing it is a pure administrative loss, and you have now done that once.

If you win a place, everything in this document about salary floors and SOC codes stops mattering for two years.

## Scale-up visa

Still requires a sponsor, but a different kind — an approved scale-up company. Sponsorship only needs to cover the first 6 months, after which you can move employers freely. Genuinely relevant given your interest in startups and tech, and worth flagging separately in the pipeline. Check the current salary threshold before relying on it.

## High Potential Individual

For graduates of universities ranked in the global top 50, within the last 5 years. Your MSc completed January 2024, so you are inside the time window. **But YOUR_UNIVERSITY typically sits outside the global top 50** in the rankings used, so you are probably not eligible. Worth ten minutes to verify against the official list for your graduation year, because if YOUR_UNIVERSITY did appear, this route needs no sponsor at all.

## Global Talent

**Not viable for you, and I would not spend time on it.** It requires endorsement from a designated body, and the endorsing bodies cover arts and culture, sciences and research, and digital technology. There is no endorsing route for business analysis, management consulting or programme delivery. Any service telling you otherwise is selling something.

## Graduate visa

Already used and expired. Not available again.

# PART 5 — Revised pipeline rules

Replace the salary logic in the build spec with this.

```
FOR UK ROLES:

1. INFER THE SOC CODE from the job title and duties. Use the mapping in
   PART 3. When ambiguous between an analyst code (2431) and a support
   code (3543), flag it as SOC-RISK.

2. IF inferred code is 3xxx, 4xxx or 5xxx:
       flag "NOT RQF6 - likely ineligible for sponsorship"
       rank bottom, do not delete (may still suit an IYPS route)

3. IF inferred code is 1xxx or 2xxx:
       floor_new_entrant  = MAX(30960, 0.70 × going_rate)
       floor_experienced  = MAX(41700, going_rate)

       salary >= floor_experienced  -> PASS, "viable any time"
       salary >= floor_new_entrant  -> PASS, "NEW ENTRANT ONLY,
                                        visa must be submitted before
                                        23 Feb 2027"
       salary <  floor_new_entrant  -> DELETE
       salary not stated            -> PASS, "ask recruiter early"

4. ADD COLUMNS: SOC Code, Going Rate, New Entrant Floor, SOC Risk

5. RANK UP any role whose description uses analysis / advisory /
   recommendation language (2431 territory) over one using support /
   coordination / administration language (3543 territory), even where
   the job titles are identical.

6. TAG separately any role at an approved scale-up sponsor.

NOTE: healthcare and education roles use national pay scales rather than
these going rates. Flag those for manual checking.
```
